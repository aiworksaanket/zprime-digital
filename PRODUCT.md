# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Founders and marketing leads at growing businesses — often India-based, often comparing agencies after a long day. They arrive slightly skeptical, scanning for someone who actually runs ads rather than someone who bought a template. The job is to decide whether Zprime is worth a strategy call.

## Product Purpose

Zprime Digital is a Pune performance-marketing studio. The site exists to explain the offer in one sitting and convert a visitor into a free audit request. Success is a qualified inquiry — not pageviews, not a brochure.

## Positioning

Marketing, computed for growth.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: Book a free audit / Get a strategy call. Secondary: See what we do.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Marketing, computed for growth.
- Belief ladder: this is a serious studio, not a template agency → they run a full funnel as one system → they report on leads, not vanity → a small team will actually touch the account → asking for an audit is low-risk.
- Proof on hand: none yet. No testimonials, case studies, press, or logos. Do not invent metrics. Proof is process, platform fluency, and the way numbers are treated (honest placeholders, not fake lift).

## Brand Personality

Precise, accountable, computed. Voice is plain-spoken and slightly dry — a media plan, not a pep talk. The interface should feel like a silver-gelatin studio that also prints spreadsheets: confident, quiet, and unwilling to shout.

## Anti-references

Neon purple/blue agency templates. Rocket-ship icons and clip-art illustrations. Gradient CTAs. Hero-metric cards with invented percentages. Stock-photo team energy. Identical icon-plus-heading card grids. Glassmorphism used as decoration.

## Design Principles

- Practice what you preach: treat numbers as accountable, never decorative.
- Restraint is competence: whitespace and type do the selling.
- One system, not five vendors: every section should read as connected, not a menu of services.
- Hands-on, not handed-off: the human scale of the studio should be visible without becoming corporate.
- Proof over posture: if a claim cannot be sourced, leave the space honest.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, placeholders treated as text. Visible focus rings. Reduced motion is not optional — every animation has an instant or crossfade fallback. Touch targets stay usable on a phone. No information is hover-only.
